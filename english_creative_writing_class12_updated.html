Notice Writing

Word limit: 50 words · Box mein likhein · Short Composition

FORMAT

Notice ka Sahi Format

Step-by-step samjho
▼
Steps
Tips
Template
  • 1 Organisation ka naam — Bold, centre mein. Jaise: "The Lotus Valley School, Noida"
  • 2 NOTICE — Capital letters mein, centre-aligned, underline karo
  • 3 Date — Left side mein. Jaise: "15 July 2026"
  • 4 Heading — Catchy, underline karo. Jaise: "Annual Science Exhibition"
  • 5 Body — Who, What, When, Where, Why — sab 3-4 lines mein
  • 6 Naam + Designation — Neeche left mein. Jaise: "Priya Sharma, Head Girl"

📦 Box Zaroori

Notice hamesha box ke andar likhein — yeh marks ke liye important hai.

🔢 50 Words

"Up to 50 words" — yeh limit hai, minimum nahi. 40-50 words ideal hai. Ghabrao mat.

📅 Future Tense

Aane wali event ke liye future tense use karein: "will be held"

❌ Avoid

Short forms, casual language aur "I/We" avoid karein. Formal raho.

✅ Common Topics

Sports day, lost & found, meeting, cultural event, school trip, vacancy

🎯 Marks

Format ke 2 marks, content ke 1 mark — format pe dhyan do

Invitation & Reply

Word limit: 50 words each · Formal ya Informal

FORMAT

Invitation ke 2 Types

Formal (3rd person) vs Informal (1st person)
▼
Formal
Informal
Reply karna
  • 1Third person mein likhein — "Mr. and Mrs. Sharma request the pleasure of your company..."
  • 2Occasion clearly mention karein — wedding, reception, anniversary etc.
  • 3Date, Time, Venue — neatly mention karein
  • 4RSVP — "RSVP: 9999999999" likhein neeche
Mr. and Mrs. Rajesh Sharma request the pleasure of your company at the wedding reception of their daughter Priya with Mr. Arjun Mehta on Saturday, 14 February 2026 at 7:00 pm Hotel Grand, Connaught Place, New Delhi RSVP: 9876543210
  • 1First person mein — "Dear Sneha, I would love to invite you..."
  • 2Friendly, warm tone rakho — jaise dost ko likh rahe ho
  • 3Date, time, place clearly bolo
  • 4"Do come!" ya "Looking forward to seeing you" se close karo
15 January 2026 Dear Ananya, I am so excited to invite you to my birthday party on 20 January 2026 at 6:00 pm at my home (A-45, Green Park, New Delhi). It would be so lovely to celebrate with you. Please do come! Yours lovingly, Riya

✅ Accept karna

Same tone mein thank karo. "We/I accept with great pleasure..." clearly likhein.

❌ Decline karna

Wajah dijiye, regret express karein. "Regret that I am unable to attend due to..."

— Formal Accept — Mr. and Mrs. Vivek Gupta thank Mr. and Mrs. Rajesh Sharma for their kind invitation to the wedding reception of their daughter Priya on 14 February 2026 and have great pleasure in accepting the same. — Informal Decline — 16 January 2026 Dear Riya, Thank you so much for inviting me to your birthday party. Unfortunately, I have my tuition examination on that day and will not be able to attend. I am really sorry. Wishing you a very happy birthday! Yours, Ananya

Letter Writing

120–150 words · Job Application ya Letter to Editor

FORMAT

Letter ka Complete Format

2 types: Job Application + Letter to Editor
▼
Format
Job Letter
Bio-Data ⭐
To Editor
Key Tips
  • 1Sender ka Address — Top left mein (apna address)
  • 2Date — Address ke neeche
  • 3Receiver ka Address — Editor/HR Manager ka naam + address
  • 4Subject — 1 line mein, underline karo. "Subject: Application for..."
  • 5Salutation — "Sir/Ma'am" ya "Respected Sir/Ma'am"
  • 6Body — 3 paragraphs: Introduction → Main Points → Conclusion
  • 7Closing — "Yours faithfully" ya "Yours sincerely" + Naam
A-12, Sector 15 Noida — 201301 15 June 2026 The HR Manager Tech Solutions Ltd. Connaught Place, New Delhi — 110001 Subject: Application for the Post of Junior Developer Respected Sir/Ma'am, With reference to your advertisement in The Hindu dated 10 June 2026, I wish to apply for the post of Junior Developer in your esteemed organisation. I am a graduate in Computer Science from Delhi University with 85% marks. I have good knowledge of Python, Java and Web Development. I am a quick learner and a team player. I am enclosing my bio-data for your kind perusal. I request you to give me an opportunity to prove my worth. I look forward to a positive response. Yours faithfully, Vikram Sharma ── BIO-DATA ────────────────────────── Name : Vikram Sharma Father's Name: Mr. Rajiv Sharma Date of Birth: 12 March 2005 Education : B.Sc (CS), 85% — Delhi Uni. Skills : Python, Java, HTML/CSS Experience : 6-month internship at ABC Tech References : Available on request

⭐ CBSE Syllabus mein clearly "Application for a Job with Bio-Data or Resume" likha hai

Bio-Data word count letter mein count nahi hota — alag se likhein. Letter + Bio-Data dono zaroori hain.

  • 1Career Objective — 1-2 lines. Aap kya banna chahte ho, kyun is field mein aana chahte ho.
  • 2Personal Details — Naam, DOB, Address, Contact, Email
  • 3Educational Qualifications — Latest pehle. Class, School/College, % ya Grade
  • 4Skills — Technical + Soft skills dono (e.g. MS Office, Communication, Leadership)
  • 5Experience — Internship, volunteer work, projects (agar koi ho)
  • 6Declaration + Date + Signature — "I hereby declare that the above information is true..."
── BIO-DATA ────────────────────────────── CAREER OBJECTIVE To obtain a position as a content writer where I can utilise my writing skills and creativity to contribute meaningfully to the organisation. PERSONAL DETAILS Name : Nandini Kapoor Date of Birth : 15 April 2005 Address : B-12, Pitampura, New Delhi — 110034 Contact : 9876543210 Email : nandini.kapoor@email.com EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATIONS Class XII : Commerce, 88% — DAV Public School (2025) Class X : 91% — DAV Public School (2023) SKILLS Technical : MS Word, MS Excel, Canva, Basic HTML Languages : English (Fluent), Hindi (Native) Soft Skills : Communication, Teamwork, Time Management EXPERIENCE Blog Writing : Personal blog — 200+ articles (2023–present) Volunteer : School Magazine Editor, Class XI–XII DECLARATION I hereby declare that all the above information is true and correct to the best of my knowledge. Date: 10 June 2026 Place: New Delhi Nandini Kapoor

💡 Bio-Data mein Declaration zaroori hai — bina iske incomplete maana jaata hai. Declaration ke marks alag se milte hain.

B-7, Model Town New Delhi — 110009 20 July 2026 The Editor The Hindustan Times New Delhi — 110001 Subject: Rising Problem of Water Scarcity in Delhi Respected Sir/Ma'am, Through the esteemed columns of your newspaper, I wish to draw the attention of the authorities towards the acute water scarcity being faced by residents of Model Town, New Delhi. For the past two months, the water supply has been irregular and insufficient. Many families, especially the elderly, face great difficulty. The underground water level is also falling rapidly. No action has been taken despite several complaints. I request the concerned authorities to take immediate action and ensure regular water supply. People's participation in water conservation should also be encouraged. Thanking you, Yours faithfully, Priya Nair

🔑 Subject Line

Subject hamesha underline karo. Ek line mein clearly purpose batao.

✉️ Closing

"Yours faithfully" — jab receiver ka naam nahin pata. "Yours sincerely" — jab naam pata ho.

📏 Word Limit

120–150 words strictly. Bio-data word count mein count nahin hota.

💼 Job Letter

Apni qualification + skills highlight karo. Overconfident mat lago.

📰 Editor Letter

Problem → Impact → Request — yeh 3 cheezein zaroor aani chahiye.

❌ Avoid

Emotional language, too many adjectives, grammar mistakes avoid karo.

Article Writing

120–150 words · Descriptive ya Analytical

FORMAT

Article ka Sahi Structure

Catchy title se strong conclusion tak
▼
Steps
Article vs Report
Tips
  • 1TITLE — Bold, catchy, centre-aligned. Jaise: "Social Media: Boon or Bane?"
  • 2By [Aapka Naam] — Naam neeche likhein, ek line mein
  • 3Introduction (Hook) — Ek strong opening line, reader ko pakad lo
  • 4Body Para 1 — Issue/Topic explain karo, facts ya data doon
  • 5Body Para 2 — Different angles, examples, impact
  • 6Conclusion — Opinion ya call to action. Memorable line se khatam karo

📰 Article

Opinion + Analysis. Writer ki apni soch. "I believe...", "In my opinion..." allowed hai.

📊 Report

Sirf facts. Third person mein. "The event was held...", "It was reported..."

📝 Article Format

Title → By Name → 3 paras. Persuasive ya informative ho sakta hai.

📋 Report Format

Headline → Byline → Dateline → Body. News style mein. Objective.

🎣 Hook Opening

Question se shuru karo, ya shocking fact se: "Did you know India produces 9.4 million tonnes of plastic every year?"

💬 Language

Expressive but formal. "Furthermore," "However," "It is evident that" jaise connectors use karo.

🔢 Figures

Statistics se article zyada convincing lagta hai. Koi bhi relevant data zaroor daalein.

📏 Word Count

120–150 words mein rakho. Title aur "By Name" count hota hai, dhyan rakho.

Report Writing

120–150 words · Event ya Incident · News Style

FORMAT

Report ka News Style Format

Journalist ki tarah likhna seekho
▼
Steps
Template
Tips
  • 1HEADLINE — Bold, news-style. Jaise: "Annual Sports Day Celebrated with Great Enthusiasm"
  • 2By-line — "By [Aapka Naam] | [City], [Date]"
  • 3Opening line — Sabse important info pehle — What happened, where, when
  • 4Body — Details: participants, activities, highlights, quotes (optional)
  • 5Conclusion — Vote of thanks, overall tone, closing line

⚠️ Yaad rakho

Report hamesha third person mein likhein. "The event was held...", "It was attended by..." — "I" ka use kabhi mat karo.

HEADLINE: EVENT KA NAAM CLEARLY LIKHO By [Aapka Naam] | [City], [Date] [Opening line: What happened, where, when — ek sentence mein sab kuch] [Para 1: Event ka description — kaun aaya, kya hua, highlights] [Para 2: Main activities ya competitions, winners ya achievements] [Para 3: Chief guest ka address ya quote, vote of thanks] The event concluded [with / on a...] note, leaving all participants [inspired / motivated / entertained].

📰 5 W's

Who, What, When, Where, Why — yeh sab cover karna zaroori hai.

🚫 No "I"

Report mein kabhi "I attended" mat likho. Hamesha third person.

⏰ Past Tense

Har verb past tense mein hoga — "was held", "were present", "delivered".

💬 Quote

Chief guest ka ek quote daalein agar possible ho — marks milenge.
